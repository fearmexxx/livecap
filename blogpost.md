🚀 How to Add More Blogs (3 Simple Steps)
Adding blogs is the fastest way to boost your SEO. Follow this pattern:

Step 1: Register the Post
Open src/app/blog/page.tsx and add a new object to the blogPosts array at the top.

Slug: Use lowercase with hyphens (e.g., future-of-events-hcmc). This is your URL.
Image: Use any image from the public folder.
Step 2: Add the Content
Open src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx and add your content to the blogContent object.

The Key must match the Slug you created in Step 1.
Use <p>, <h2>, and <blockquote> tags to keep the layout premium.
Step 3: SEO Optimization
Make sure your title and excerpt contain the keywords you want to rank for (e.g., "Event Production Saigon", "Brand Activation University").
